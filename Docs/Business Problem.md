\## Project

\*\*Indian Retail Sales \& Profitability Analysis\*\*



\## Business Context



The company operates across multiple products, customer segments, and geographic markets in India.



Management has historical sales data but lacks a clear understanding of which parts of the business are driving profitable growth and which areas require attention.



High sales do not necessarily mean high profitability, so management needs to evaluate revenue, profit, margins, products, markets, customers, and discounting together.



\## Business Problem



Management needs to understand:



> \*\*What is driving or limiting sales and profitability, and where should the business focus its efforts to improve profitable growth?\*\*



The analysis will investigate performance across time, products, geographic markets, customer segments, discounts, and available operational data.



\## Business Objectives



The project aims to:



\- Evaluate overall sales and profitability performance.

\- Identify important sales and profit trends.

\- Find high-performing and underperforming products.

\- Compare performance across regions and states.

\- Evaluate customer segment performance.

\- Determine how discounting relates to profitability.

\- Investigate major increases or declines through root-cause analysis.

\- Provide actionable recommendations to management.



\## Key Business Questions



1\. How have sales, profit, and profit margin changed over time?

2\. Which categories, subcategories, and products drive the most revenue and profit?

3\. Which products generate high sales but relatively weak profitability?

4\. Which regions and states perform strongest and weakest?

5\. How do customer segments differ in sales and profitability?

6\. How does discounting relate to sales and profit margins?

7\. What factors explain major changes in business performance?

8\. Where should management focus to improve profitable growth?



\## Key KPIs



\- Total Sales

\- Total Profit

\- Profit Margin %

\- Quantity Sold

\- Order / Transaction Count

\- Average Order Value

\- Average Discount %

\- Sales Growth %

\- Profit Growth %

\- Average Shipping Days, where supported by the data



\## Stakeholders



\*\*Primary stakeholders:\*\*



\- Head of Sales

\- Finance Manager / CFO

\- Regional Business Managers



\## Scope



The project will primarily cover:



\*\*Time → Product → Geography → Customer Segment → Discount → Profitability\*\*



Operational analysis will be included where the available data supports reliable conclusions.



The project will not make claims about customer satisfaction, churn, delivery quality, or causation unless the dataset provides sufficient evidence.
